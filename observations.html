Observations

—
A weekly review of the observations-dependent decisions built into the VirtualDentist system. Each section shows the current signal, what it means, and what to do if the numbers are trending the wrong way. The aim is to close loose ends with data rather than remember them — review this page once a week and take action on anything marked WARN or BAD.
Adding a new observation: when a new observation-dependent decision is introduced (e.g., "did the regen drift fix work?"), add a section to this page. The pattern is: explainer → query → status calculation → suggested action. The cost of one more observation should stay near zero.

Loose-end principle: any observation whose signal is WARN or BAD for more than 14 days without resolution indicates that either the prompt-only fix isn't working, or the decision has been forgotten. Both are signals to revisit.
