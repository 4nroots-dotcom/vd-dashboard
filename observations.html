Observations

—
A weekly review of the observations-dependent decisions built into the system. Each section shows the current signal, what it means, and what to do if numbers trend the wrong way. The point is to close loose ends with data rather than remember them — open this page once a week and take action on anything marked WARN or BAD.
Adding a new observation: when a new observation-dependent decision is introduced, add a section to this page. The pattern is: explainer → query → status calculation → suggested action. Cost of adding one more observation should stay near zero.

Loose-end principle: any observation whose signal is WARN or BAD for more than 14 days without resolution means either the prompt-only fix isn't working, or the decision has been forgotten — both are signals to revisit.
