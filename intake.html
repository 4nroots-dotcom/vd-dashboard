Why we built this.
Dental concerns don't wait for office hours. But most dental clinics aren't set up to take new information at 11pm on a Saturday. You're often stuck Googling symptoms or waiting it out until Monday.
We built this so your concern can be reviewed by our dental team — even when our doors are closed. You tell us what's happening, Sam gathers structured details, and our dental team reviews it. No guesswork on your end about what to share. No waiting on hold.
And yes, it's free. There's no charge for the triage assessment. If you need to come in for treatment, that's a separate conversation — and we'll walk you through costs before anything happens.
