Why we built this.
Dental emergencies don't wait for office hours. But most dental clinics aren't set up to help you at 11pm on a Saturday. You're stuck Googling symptoms, debating whether to go to the ER, or just waiting it out in pain until Monday.
We built this triage service so you can get your concern in front of a real dentist — even when our doors are closed. You tell us what's happening, Sam gathers the clinical details, and a dentist reviews it all. No guesswork. No waiting on hold. No unnecessary ER visit for something a dentist should handle.
And yes, it's free. There's no charge for the triage assessment. If you need to come in for treatment, that's a separate conversation — and we'll walk you through costs before anything happens.
Not sure if it counts as an emergency? Start here anyway.
Our triage service handles a wide range of dental concerns, including:
Severe or persistent toothache Broken, cracked, or chipped teeth Knocked-out (avulsed) tooth Dental abscess or signs of infection Facial or gum swelling Lost filling, crown, or restoration Injury to the mouth, jaw, or teeth Post-procedure pain or complications
If you're not sure whether your situation qualifies, start the assessment anyway. That's exactly what the triage is for.
