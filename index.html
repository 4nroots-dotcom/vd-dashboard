How it works

Three steps.
Two minutes.

From concern to clinical triage — without waiting on hold or coming into the office first.

01
Fill out a short form
Describe your concern in your own words. Share a few details about your symptoms. Takes about two minutes and can be done from your phone.
02
Sam calls you
Sam, our AI dental triage assistant, calls you back and asks guided clinical questions — helping assess the nature and urgency of your concern.
03
A dentist reviews your case
A licensed Ontario dentist reviews the structured triage note and may follow up with you directly during office hours with guidance and next steps.
