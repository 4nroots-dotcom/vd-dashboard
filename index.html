Your team stays in control

Sam structures the call. Your team makes the calls.

Coverage is only worth having if you can trust it. So Sam stays inside a hard boundary: it never diagnoses, never assesses urgency, and never makes a clinical decision. When a patient describes a clinical concern, Sam captures what they reported, structures it, and routes it to your practice — and your dentist and staff decide what happens next, with a clear picture already in front of them. The software does the gathering; the clinical judgment stays with the people licensed to make it. That's what lets you put Sam in front of your practice and stand behind it.

After-hours coverage that builds your practice
Every after-hours call becomes a handled case — not a missed opportunity on voicemail. Patients get an answer; your team gets a clean handoff in the morning. Need to step in yourself? Review and message from your phone, anytime.

Secure patient messaging
Message patients from the dashboard — your front desk by day, or you personally at 11pm. Everything runs through a dedicated practice number, never a personal phone. Two-way threads are logged alongside the case.

All patient data is stored in a Canadian-region database. Personal health information is stripped before any data leaves Canadian infrastructure.

VirtualDentist.ai is an AI receptionist and structured intake software for dental practices. It does not provide clinical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and does not make clinical decisions. Clinical concerns are structured and routed to the practice's own clinical team, who make all clinical decisions. The software does not replace emergency medical care or an in-person dental examination.
