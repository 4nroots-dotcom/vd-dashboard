How it works

Patient concern to clinical note
in three steps.

1
Patient submits a concern
From your website, a QR code in your office, an embedded widget, or a Google Ads landing page. The patient describes what's happening in their own words.
2
Sam triages
Sam calls (English) or texts (other languages) the patient with guided clinical questions — pain, swelling, trauma, red flags. The triage takes about 3 minutes.
3
Your team gets the note
A structured clinical note lands in your dashboard with severity classification, clinical flags, recommended disposition, and the patient's contact info. Your team follows up.
Platform

What the platform does.

🩸
Clinical-grade triage
30+ clinical rules covering endodontic diagnostics (cold lingering, heat sensitivity, nocturnal pain), infection screening, trauma assessment, and periapical pathology detection. Severity is computed, not guessed.
🌐
9 languages via SMS
English, Arabic, Hindi, Punjabi, French, Farsi, Somali, Spanish, and Chinese. Non-English patients are triaged via text message in their language. The clinical note is always in English.
🕑
24/7 coverage
Sam takes cases nights, weekends, and holidays. After-hours cases are queued for review first thing the next business day. Urgent and emergency cases trigger immediate alerts.
📈
Every concern, one platform
From dental emergencies to routine appointment requests. Sam handles the full spectrum — urgent triage, after-hours intake, general inquiries, insurance questions, and new patient onboarding.
🔒
Patient data stays in Canada
All patient data is stored in a Canadian-region database. PHI is stripped before any cross-border API call. No patient health information leaves Canadian infrastructure.
🔨
Embeddable anywhere
Drop-in integration block for your existing website, iframe widget for contact pages, standalone landing pages for ad campaigns, and QR codes for in-office intake. One platform, multiple entry points.
