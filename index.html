Your team stays in control

Sam structures the call. Your team makes the calls.

Coverage is only worth having if you can trust it. So Sam stays inside a hard boundary: it never diagnoses and never makes a clinical decision — and any prioritization of incoming cases is rule-based, configured with your practice, never an AI judgment call. When a patient describes a clinical concern, Sam captures what they reported, structures it, and routes it to your practice — and your dentist and staff decide what happens next, with a clear picture already in front of them. The software does the gathering; the clinical judgment stays with the people licensed to make it. That's what lets you put Sam behind your practice and stand behind it.

VirtualDentist.ai is an AI receptionist and structured intake software for dental practices. It does not provide clinical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and does not make clinical decisions. Clinical concerns are structured and routed to the practice's own clinical team, who make all clinical decisions. The software does not replace emergency medical care or an in-person dental examination.
† "1 in 5" is measured from production call data at a live Canadian dental practice: 12.8% of calls arrive outside open hours, and a further ~5% arrive while another call is already underway (open 45 of the week's 168 hours). The overnight log shown above is an illustrative sample.
